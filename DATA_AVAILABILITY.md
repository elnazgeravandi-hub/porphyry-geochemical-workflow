# Data Availability

The original geochemical dataset and associated restricted spatial data are subject to data-owner restrictions. The authors do not have permission to publish or redistribute these data; therefore, they are not included in this repository.

Exact reproduction of the manuscript’s numerical results requires authorized access to the original inputs. Public availability of the analysis code does not remove these data-access restrictions.

Fully synthetic demonstration inputs can be generated using `examples/synthetic_demo/run.py`. The example enables execution and testing of the computational workflow without access to the restricted study inputs. Synthetic records and abstract spatial supports do not represent the study area, reproduce the manuscript's numerical results or validate its geological conclusions.
